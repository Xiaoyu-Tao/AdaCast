Evaluate adjusted predictions separately from raw eval.json. Use this after `forecast_adjustment_apply`. The evaluation is bound to `apply-results.json` and records an audited `application_summary`. The validation MSE-gain floor is fixed at -100%, and legacy values are normalized to that floor. A frozen `user_prior_mirror` with finite artifacts and a passing leakage audit is always retainable, including zero-trigger and metric-regression cases; ordinary candidates still apply MAE/WAPE guardrails. Test evaluations report adjusted metrics only after the skill is already locked; do not use them to revise candidates. This tool writes `adjusted_eval.json` under `.forecast/adjustments/` and never overwrites raw run metrics.
