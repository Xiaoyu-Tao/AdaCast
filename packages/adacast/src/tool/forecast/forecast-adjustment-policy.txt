Configure and inspect the optional deterministic generative prediction reasoning policy. Use this before generating adjustment candidates. The validation MSE-gain floor is fixed at -100%; a valid expert-prior mirror with a passing leakage audit is retained regardless of held-out metric change, while ordinary candidates keep MAE/WAPE guardrails. The remaining policy fields control guardrails, candidate budgets, and maximum point delta ratio. The policy cannot change the task-level fixed validation-front evidence reservation or bypass user-facing generative prediction reasoning choices. For user-facing choices, including whether to use lightweight prediction reasoning, use deep prediction reasoning, or apply a locked reasoning skill in the final test stage, use `forecast_adjustment_decision`; `confirm_for_critic` is retained only as a legacy state confirmation helper. This tool writes only `.forecast/adjustments/policy.json` and the adjustment summary in `.forecast/STATE.md`; it never changes raw prediction artifacts.
