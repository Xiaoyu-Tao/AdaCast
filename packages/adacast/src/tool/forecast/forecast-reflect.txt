Record a structured reflection on the most recent experiment round.

This tool MUST be called after every experiment round (generate_model + evaluate_experiment). It appends a ReflectionRecord to .forecast/history.jsonl that captures whether the experiment improved on the current best, why, what to try next, and the outcome decision.

The forecast_state tool will block the next experiment round if the most recent ExperimentRecord in history.jsonl does not have a paired ReflectionRecord.

Parameters:
- run_id: The spec hash (run_id) of the experiment being reflected on
- effective: true if the experiment improved on the current best result, false otherwise
- reason: Concise explanation of why the experiment succeeded or failed
- next_direction: What to try next and why, based on what was learned
- decision: One of "keep" (update best), "discard" (no improvement), "crash" (runner failure), "stop" (terminate loop)
